## What HYSN does

HYSN provides full-service Digital Signage for professional locations — restaurants, retailers, supermarkets, franchise chains, events, municipalities, and public spaces.

The core offer is **not hardware alone**. It is a complete system:

- Consulting and site analysis
- Hardware (LCD displays, LED video walls, digital steles, touch solutions)
- Content creation and animation (screen-ready, not just static graphics)
- Installation and technical setup (mounting, cabling, media player, CMS)
- Ongoing operation and support (content updates, maintenance, fault response)

**One-liner (approved, use verbatim in copy):**
> Wir helfen Restaurants, Einzelhändlern, Standortpartnern und Kommunen, ihre Flächen mit digitalen Displays zu modernisieren und sichtbarer zu kommunizieren — ohne dass sie Hardware, Content, Installation und Betrieb selbst koordinieren müssen.

---

## The problem HYSN solves

Customers who want digital screens typically face fragmented responsibility: one vendor for hardware, another for mounting, another for content, another for support. When something goes wrong, no single party owns the outcome.

HYSN exists because this gap was visible in the market. Its value is a single point of responsibility across the entire project lifecycle.

## Values (as daily behaviour, not poster words)

1. **Komplettverantwortung** — One contact for the whole project. HYSN does not hand off and disappear after delivery.
2. **Technik, die zum Standort passt** — Site conditions (light, viewing distance, usage, budget) determine the recommendation, not the other way around.
3. **Content wird nicht nachträglich angehängt** — Design, animation and content planning are built into the solution from the start.
4. **Erreichbarkeit nach der Inbetriebnahme** — Prices change, campaigns end, content needs updating. HYSN remains reachable after go-live.

## 4-step project process

| Step | Title | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Standort besprechen | Number of surfaces, photos or floor plan, desired content, usage goal and operating model |
| 02 | Setup empfehlen | Display types, sizes and mounting, content format, operating concept |
| 03 | Content vorbereiten | Menus and offers designed, campaigns built, formatted for the specific screens |
| 04 | Installieren & betreiben | Mounting, setup, pre-go-live test, ongoing content management (optional), technical support |

---

## Brand and voice rules

- **Tone:** customer-as-hero. HYSN is the guide, not the hero.
- **Address:** Sie-form throughout (formal German).
- **Opener to avoid:** never start with "Wir sind HYSN" or equivalent self-referential openers.
- **Headline principle:** tell the reader what HYSN means for *their* project — responsibility, reliability, one contact.
- **Claims:** specific over generic. "30+ Screens im hyads-Netz" beats "years of experience."
